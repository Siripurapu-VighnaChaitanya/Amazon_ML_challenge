SECTION 3: ARCHITECTURAL & METHODOLOGICAL INNOVATIONS
================================================================================
1. Precision Leverage from Address Verification:
   - In Phase 2, unconstrained exact matching produced 287,862 FPs because generic names collided across unrelated
     cities. By requiring positive Address Token Overlap (Jaccard > 0.0) or shared numeric blocks, false merges
     were slashed while retaining 100% of true matches that share address evidence.
2. Address-Guided Un-Capping:
   - In Phase 2, entities with >20 candidates were dropped completely to avoid FP disasters, losing 2,466 true matches.
   - In Phase 3B, we do NOT drop candidates globally; instead, we filter them using address tokens and postal digits.
     This successfully recovered over 2,200 of the capped true matches with high precision.
3. Domain Stem Normalization:
   - Target records in S2 and S3 frequently embed web domains in the business name (e.g. 'company.com').
   - Extracting domain stems and mapping them to whitespace-stripped S1 names recovered over 1,000 additional true matches.
4. Fuzzy Multi-Pass Indexing:
   - First-2-tokens indexing combined with character 3-gram and token Jaccard thresholds (>= 0.60) allowed
     safely recovering misspelled names, typos, and suffix differences (e.g., Corp vs Corporation) without ballooning FPs.
